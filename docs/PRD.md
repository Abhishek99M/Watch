# Product requirements

Create a premium, minimal, cinematic and engineering-focused watch website. The narrative covers opening hero, details, exploded watch, movement, materials, craftsmanship, reassembly, configuration, product information and brand story. Add cart and checkout in their scheduled phases.

Provide responsive navigation, useful content without WebGL, keyboard controls, visible focus, reduced motion, loading progress and safe recoverable errors.

Do not invent materials, movement specifications, water resistance, dimensions, warranty, prices or inventory. Approved product data, licensed GLB assets, payment provider and production domain are still needed.

Source: the user-supplied 3D Watch Website Complete Codex Build, Test & Security Guide. Its phase order and release gates govern implementation.
